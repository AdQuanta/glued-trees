## Scope and technical content

Uses near-surface NV centers to probe a two-dimensional dipolar electron-spin ensemble through decoherence and double-electron-electron-resonance spectroscopy. Stretch exponents, correlation times, and driven-bath response reveal dimensionality, disorder, and many-body dynamics, turning probe dephasing into a quantitative noise spectrum.
