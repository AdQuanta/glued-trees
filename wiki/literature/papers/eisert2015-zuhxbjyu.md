## Scope and technical content

Synthesizes equilibration and thermalization in local quantum many-body systems through locality bounds, typicality, the eigenstate thermalization hypothesis, transport, many-body localization, ramps, and periodic driving. It explains why local observables may equilibrate without the global state becoming stationary and why long-time classical simulation is generically hard.
