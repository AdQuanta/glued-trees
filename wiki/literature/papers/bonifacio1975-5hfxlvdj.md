## Scope and technical content

Derives a master equation and generalized Maxwell-Bloch equations for cooperative emission from an initially inverted, incoherent two-level ensemble with inhomogeneous broadening. A pendulum reduction identifies geometry and dephasing thresholds for superfluorescence, pulse delay, sech-shaped bursts, and the crossover to stimulated oscillatory emission.
