## Main result

It derives exact classes of N-pair eigenstates whose energy is a sum of pair energies satisfying coupled algebraic equations, providing the Richardson roots underlying later integrable quench dynamics.

## Mathematical core

The reduced pairing model is commonly represented as

$$
H=\sum_j2\epsilon_jS_j^z-g\sum_{j,k}S_j^+S_k^-.
$$

Richardson constructs an $N$-pair eigenstate from pair-creation operators parametrized by roots $E_\alpha$, with total energy $E=\sum_\alpha E_\alpha$; the roots obey coupled algebraic equations whose exact coefficients depend on the chosen pairing convention. The essential result is that the interacting many-pair problem remains exactly solvable through those roots rather than through an uncontrolled mean-field closure.
