## Scope and technical content

Introduces continuous-time quantum walks as Hamiltonian evolution generated by a graph Laplacian and applies them to decision trees. The paper's tree example gives rapid quantum propagation but is not yet the later black-box lower-bound separation; it establishes the dynamical primitive and the importance of the invariant symmetric subspace.
