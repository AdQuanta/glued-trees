## Scope and technical content

Combines BCS-like electron-hole pairing with a Bethe-Salpeter propagator to calculate pump-probe spectra from dilute excitonic Bose condensation to dense electron-hole BCS pairing. State filling, band-gap renormalization, and pair correlations create sharp collective phase-mode sidebands that evolve continuously into exciton resonances.
