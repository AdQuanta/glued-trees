## Scope and technical content

Reviews continuous-time quantum walks as coherent transport models on finite and complex networks, using transition amplitudes, return probabilities, long-time averages, and spectral densities. It covers regular lattices, fractal and small-world structures, long-range links, traps, and static disorder, making clear that spectral degeneracies and topology control transport efficiency.
