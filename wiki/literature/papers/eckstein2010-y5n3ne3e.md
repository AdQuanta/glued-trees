## Scope and technical content

Uses nonequilibrium dynamical mean-field theory with a quantum Monte Carlo impurity solver for an interaction quench in the Hubbard model. Weak and strong quenches enter distinct prethermal plateaus, while an intermediate dynamical crossover thermalizes one-particle observables rapidly and forms Hubbard bands visible in spectra and optical conductivity.
