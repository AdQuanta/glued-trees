## Scope and technical content

Maps numerous correlated insulating states at integer and fractional fillings of a WSe2/WS2 moiré superlattice using a nearby exciton sensor. Their hierarchy and temperature scales agree qualitatively with a long-range triangular-lattice t–V model, extending the picture from a single Mott state to generalized Wigner crystals.
