## Scope and technical content

Models coherent exciton motion on small-world networks formed by adding random shortcuts to a ring. Extra bonds accelerate approach to the long-time distribution, yet quantum interference prevents equipartition and retains enhanced return probability at the initial node, so faster spreading and uniform transport are distinct claims.
