## Scope and technical content

Combines a Floquet treatment of strong periodic drive with Kubo linear response to a weak probe and derives a four-time Green-function/Dyson formulation of disorder scattering. The generalized Floquet golden rule includes inter-replica processes, and a continuum approximation can predict qualitatively wrong conductivity even when its spectrum resembles the lattice model.
