## Scope and technical content

Adds a marked-vertex oracle to a continuous-time walk and analyzes spatial search through the low-energy spectrum. Full quadratic search is obtained on periodic lattices above four dimensions, dimension four is logarithmically marginal, and lower dimensions do not obtain the same speedup, showing that connectivity and spectral dimension matter.
