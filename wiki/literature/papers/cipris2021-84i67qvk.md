## Scope and technical content

Studies how atomic motion driven by van der Waals forces dephases long-lived Dicke subradiant modes in cold clouds. Subradiance, because of its long storage time, is especially sensitive to interaction-induced motion; the observed decay and numerical modeling separate motional dephasing from ordinary radiative broadening.
