## Mathematical core

The weak-field polarizability contains both resonant and antiresonant response,

$$
\alpha(\omega)\propto
\frac{1}{\omega_0-\omega-i\gamma/2}
+\frac{1}{\omega_0+\omega+i\gamma/2}.
$$

Keeping the second term and the radiative linewidth prescription is what makes the response consistent with the optical theorem. The paper is a useful check against effective collective-mode models that insert damping by hand.
