## Scope and technical content

Constructs a fully quantized electrodynamics model for localized two-level systems, keeping counter-rotating and self-energy structure needed beyond a purely phenomenological qubit Hamiltonian. The analysis highlights gauge, ultraviolet, and causality issues that arise when atom-field response is truncated too aggressively.
