## Scope and technical content

Uses coupled-dipole simulations and strong-disorder renormalization to explain why a dense disordered atomic gas does not acquire an arbitrarily large refractive index. Near-field pair interactions create density-dependent inhomogeneous broadening, limiting the index to about 1.7 and the number of resonant atoms per cubic wavelength.
