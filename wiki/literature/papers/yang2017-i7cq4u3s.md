Reviews quantum many-body treatments of central-spin decoherence in nanoscale nuclear baths, especially cluster-correlation expansions. The hierarchy captures bath correlations generated during free induction, echo, and driven control and explains why a structured quantum bath cannot generally be replaced by independent classical noise.
