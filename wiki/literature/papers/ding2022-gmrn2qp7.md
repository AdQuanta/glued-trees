## Scope and technical content

Uses the sharp optical response near a nonequilibrium Rydberg critical point to sense microwave fields. The inferred Fisher information increases by roughly three orders of magnitude over a single-particle response, while critical slowing down limits how quickly the stationary susceptibility can be exploited.
