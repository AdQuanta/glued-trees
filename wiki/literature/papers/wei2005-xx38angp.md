## Scope and technical content

Demonstrates a two-logical-qubit decoherence-free encoding in a four-spin NMR system by using two-dimensional multiple-quantum coherences to distinguish otherwise unresolved methyl protons. The experiment shows broader protection than a conventional encoding with four spectrally resolved spins and illustrates how symmetry-unaddressable degrees of freedom can become an encoding resource.
