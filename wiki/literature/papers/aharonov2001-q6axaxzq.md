## Scope and technical content

Develops discrete-time quantum walks on general graphs with an explicit coin degree of freedom and defines mixing, sampling, and spreading measures suited to unitary dynamics. It proves nearly quadratic improvements on cycles for several measures while bounding what this framework alone can guarantee on arbitrary graphs.
