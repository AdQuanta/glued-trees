## Scope and technical content

Uses hybrid excitons in a MoSe2/hBN/MoSe2 structure as an optical probe of strongly correlated electrons in an adjacent moiré lattice. Interaction-induced spectral shifts and incompressible features show that an excitonic sensor can read charge correlations while remaining spatially separated from the electronic system.
