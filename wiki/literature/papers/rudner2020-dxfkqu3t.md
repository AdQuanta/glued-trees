## Mathematical core

For $H(t+T)=H(t)$, a Floquet mode satisfies

$$
\bigl[H(t)-i\partial_t\bigr]|u_\alpha(t)\rangle
=\varepsilon_\alpha|u_\alpha(t)\rangle,
\qquad |u_\alpha(t+T)\rangle=|u_\alpha(t)\rangle.
$$

The quasienergy spectrum organizes band engineering but does not determine a many-body measurement by itself: occupations, micromotion, and heating channels enter separately. That distinction parallels the project’s need to connect a spectrum and a leakage mechanism to an actual exit observable.
