## Scope and technical content

Demonstrates a potassium magnetometer in the spin-exchange-relaxation-free regime by combining high density with near-zero magnetic field. Rapid exchange locks the hyperfine manifolds so relaxation is only second order in field, producing a 1.1 Hz linewidth and 10 fT/√Hz measured sensitivity with a much lower projected shot-noise floor.
