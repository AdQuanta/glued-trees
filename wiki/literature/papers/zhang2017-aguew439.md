## Scope and technical content

Uses up to 53 trapped-ion spins with tunable long-range Ising couplings and site-resolved readout to observe a dynamical transition after a field quench. Time-averaged magnetization, domain statistics, and high-order correlations expose behavior inaccessible to simple short-range or mean-field descriptions at that size.
