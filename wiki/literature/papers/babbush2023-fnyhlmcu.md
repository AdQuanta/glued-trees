## Scope and technical content

Maps the second-order equations of coupled classical oscillators to first-order Schrödinger evolution with polylogarithmic overhead under sparse oracle access. It proves exponential query separations for suitable global observables and BQP-completeness, while stating important limitations from state preparation, readout, locality, and evolution time; the glued-tree reduction becomes ballistic wave propagation.
