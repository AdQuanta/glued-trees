## Scope and technical content

Uses single photons and interferometric networks to test adjacency/Laplacian equivalence on five- and six-vertex irregular graphs. The measured distributions verify the predicted localized-start equivalence and extend it to selected superposition inputs, while also demonstrating experimentally that the equivalence fails for generic initial states.
