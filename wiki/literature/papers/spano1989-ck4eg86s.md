## Scope and technical content

Treats aggregate fluorescence as superradiance restricted to the N chromophores within each molecular aggregate. Random inhomogeneous broadening quenches enhancement when its width becomes comparable to nearest-neighbor dipole coupling, whereas homogeneous dephasing competes directly with radiative decay; the distinction is central to protection mechanisms.
