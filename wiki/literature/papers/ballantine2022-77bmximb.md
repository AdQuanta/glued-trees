## Scope and technical content

Analyzes driven arrays of strontium atoms whose electric- and magnetic-dipole collective modes can be mixed and addressed by level shifts. Cooperative interference enables optical magnetism, directional scattering, and programmable wavefront control, demonstrating that geometry can select bright and subradiant radiative eigenmodes.
