## Scope and technical content

Studies random phases in nearest-neighbor Ising-type transfer networks representing optical and nanoscale devices. Overall randomness in a two-dimensional network produces classical-like spreading, one-dimensional propagation produces exponential localization, and intermediate geometries interpolate between them, illustrating that the disorder effect depends on network architecture.
