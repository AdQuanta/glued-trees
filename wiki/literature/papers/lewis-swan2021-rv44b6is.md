## Scope and technical content

Proposes a cavity-QED simulator whose collective spin Hamiltonian realizes reduced BCS pairing dynamics. Cavity observables and controllable inhomogeneous fields make the three collisionless BCS dynamical phases accessible, connecting Anderson pseudospins, collective light coupling, and quench spectroscopy.
