## Scope and technical content

Implements the column-reduced glued-tree Hamiltonian in laser-written waveguide arrays and measures heralded single-photon transport for depths up to 16 and branching rates up to five. The optimal quantum hitting efficiency decays polynomially with depth while the classical benchmark decays exponentially, but the device realizes the symmetry-reduced chain rather than the exponentially large random oracle graph.
