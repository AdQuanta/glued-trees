## Scope and technical content

Uses QED coupled-cluster calculations and a reduced Tavis-Cummings–Kasha model to study one perturbed molecule inside a collectively coupled molecular environment. Competition between short-range Coulomb and long-range photonic correlations can invert local transition density and modify the first solvation shell, warning that collective coupling can produce structured local changes rather than uniform protection.
