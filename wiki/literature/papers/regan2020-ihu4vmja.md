## Scope and technical content

Uses optical detection in WSe2/WS2 moiré superlattices to identify a Mott insulator at one hole per site and generalized Wigner crystals at one-third and two-thirds filling. Spin-valley selection rules also reveal spin excitations with microsecond relaxation, demonstrating spectroscopy of charge and spin order without conventional transport.
