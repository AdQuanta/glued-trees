## Scope and technical content

Derives two-time optical response functions for dispersive, time-varying quantum materials and connects their generalized Kramers-Kronig structure to energy gain and loss. Few-level, oscillator, and strongly driven atomic examples show how frequency conversion and Floquet polaritons emerge beyond an instantaneous-permittivity description.
