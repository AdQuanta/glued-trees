## Scope and technical content

Projects weakly interacting fermions in spin-dependent traps onto long-range spin models in single-particle mode space while retaining coherence between occupation sectors. This framework explains demagnetization, spin waves, segregation, and self-rephasing and shows how local collisions generate collective real-space correlations.
