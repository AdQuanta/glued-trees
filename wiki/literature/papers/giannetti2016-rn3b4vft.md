## Scope and technical content

Reviews ultrafast spectroscopy of strongly correlated materials as a nonequilibrium decomposition of electronic, spin, lattice, and collective degrees of freedom. It connects pump-probe observables to microscopic Hubbard, dynamical mean-field, and bottleneck models while warning that multi-exponential fits alone do not identify a mechanism.
