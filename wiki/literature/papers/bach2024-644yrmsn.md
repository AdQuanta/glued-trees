## Scope and technical content

Measures time-resolved second-order coherence during superfluorescent bursts from roughly 900 atoms coupled directionally to a nanofiber. The experiment observes the build-up of collective coherence and large shot-to-shot delay fluctuations, showing that a bright intensity burst alone does not specify its quantum statistical coherence.
