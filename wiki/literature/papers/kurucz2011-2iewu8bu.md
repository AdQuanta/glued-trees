## Scope and technical content

Derives the linear spectrum of an inhomogeneously broadened spin ensemble coupled to a cavity by reducing the ensemble to a bright collective mode plus dark modes. The cavity response, avoided crossing, and linewidth depend on the full spectral density, not only its variance, and the formalism identifies which collective combinations are optically addressable.
