## Scope and technical content

Combines soft-core Rydberg interactions with a drive to turn an Ising interaction into an effective XX model and open a many-body gap above the collective spin manifold. The gap suppresses leakage while the collective dynamics generate useful squeezing, giving a close, finite-range example of interaction-enabled manifold protection under realistic decoherence.
