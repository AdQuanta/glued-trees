## Scope and technical content

Shows that periodic electronic charge order breaks exciton translational symmetry and produces an umklapp exciton-polaron resonance. The resonance energy encodes the charge-order lattice constant and exciton-electron interaction, providing a direct optical signature of Mott-like and Wigner-crystal order.
