## Scope and technical content

Tests how the integrable pairing-quench phase structure changes under integrability-breaking dispersion and interaction terms. Dephasing to stationary behavior and persistent oscillatory regimes can survive beyond the exactly solvable point, but their boundaries and long-time spectra shift, so integrability supplies a solution method rather than the sole cause of collective dynamics.
