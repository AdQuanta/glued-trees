## Scope and technical content

Derives analytic scattering states for a one-dimensional continuous-time walk with a diagonal defect and finds resonance bands of perfect and zero transmission for multiple defects. It also supplies an efficient numerical scheme for arbitrary diagonal disorder, showing that defects can be engineered as controls rather than treated only as degradation.
