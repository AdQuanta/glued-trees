## Scope and technical content

Defines a dynamical quantum phase transition through nonanalyticities in the Loschmidt return-rate function at critical real times. In the transverse-field Ising chain these Fisher-zero crossings are tied to quenches across the equilibrium critical point, but this return-probability definition is distinct from transitions in long-time order parameters.

## Main result

In the transverse-field Ising chain these Fisher-zero crossings are tied to quenches across the equilibrium critical point, but this return-probability definition is distinct from transitions in long-time order parameters.

## Mathematical core

The Loschmidt amplitude and associated extensive rate function are

$$
\mathcal G(t)=\langle\psi_0|e^{-iHt}|\psi_0\rangle,
\qquad
r(t)=-\lim_{N\to\infty}\frac1N\log|\mathcal G(t)|^2.
$$

The paper identifies dynamical critical times through nonanalyticities of $r(t)$, related to Fisher zeros in the complex-time plane. This is a rigorous definition of one kind of dynamical quantum phase transition, but it is not equivalent by definition to a transport-scaling crossover.

## Relevance to the glued-trees paper

Use this work to distinguish protected collective dynamics, prethermal plateaux, crossovers, and genuine dynamical transitions. The glued-tree claim still needs the declared large-depth transport observable.
