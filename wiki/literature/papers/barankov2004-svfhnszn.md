## Scope and technical content

Solves collisionless BCS pairing formation after switching on attraction and maps the dynamics to Anderson pseudospin precession. Exact single-soliton and soliton-train order parameters correspond to collective 2π Rabi rotations and remain robust to weak noise and damping within the pre-relaxation window.
