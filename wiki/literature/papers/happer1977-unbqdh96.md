## Scope and technical content

Develops vector, perturbative, non-Hermitian matrix, and stochastic theories of the rapid-spin-exchange spectrum. In the fast-exchange limit the precession frequency becomes a fixed fraction of the bare Larmor frequency and the linewidth scales inversely with collision rate, the opposite of the slow-exchange regime.
