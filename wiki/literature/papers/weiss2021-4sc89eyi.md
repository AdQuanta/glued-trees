## Scope and technical content

Recasts dilute-cloud linear-optics superradiance as a single scattering event embedded in the dispersive effective medium of the other atoms. The resulting decay rate agrees with coupled-dipole simulations and experiment, and Doppler broadening can be inserted to predict how detuning recovers cooperative decay in hot samples.
