## Scope and technical content

Measures high-temperature spin depolarization and multiple-quantum coherences in randomly, effectively all-to-all interacting solid-state NMR ensembles. Rescaled temporal correlations collapse to a universal functional form governed by a few parameters, demonstrating far-from-equilibrium universality without low-energy criticality.
