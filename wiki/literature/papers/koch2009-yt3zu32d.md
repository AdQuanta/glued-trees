## Scope and technical content

Proposes pump-probe photoassociation spectroscopy of ultracold gases to expose two-body correlations. A shaped pump creates correlated atom pairs and a delayed probe maps their motion, making the signal sensitive to the pair distribution and interaction potential rather than only to one-body density.
