## Scope and technical content

Computes light propagation when static long-range dipolar interactions pre-correlate emitter positions. Repulsion suppresses extreme resonant couplings, usually improving coherent reflection and optical depth and enabling selective subradiant excitation, while nonuniform interaction shifts introduce inhomogeneous broadening that can undo the cooperative gain.
