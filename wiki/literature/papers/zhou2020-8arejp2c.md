## Scope and technical content

Engineers a Floquet pulse sequence for a dense interacting NV ensemble that cancels disorder, dipolar interactions, finite pulse width, and leading control errors. The DROID-60 protocol lengthens coherence about fivefold and improves AC magnetometry beyond the interaction-limited plateau; this is active control, not autonomous Hamiltonian protection.
