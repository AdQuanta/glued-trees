## Scope and technical content

Measures time-resolved four-wave mixing from cavity polaritons coupled to a two-dimensional electron gas in the fractional quantum Hall regime. Strong ground-state correlations and exciton-electron dressing greatly enhance polariton-polariton nonlinearities, making nonlinear response a probe of fractional states that can be inconspicuous in linear spectra.

## Main result
