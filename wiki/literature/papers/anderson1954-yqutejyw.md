## Main result

In the extreme-narrowing limit the central line is Lorentzian-like with half-width of order the static mean-square frequency spread divided by the exchange or motion rate, while the wings fall faster; hyperfine components move together before merging.

## Mathematical core

The model treats local resonance frequencies as states of a stochastic exchange process with rate scale $R$. In the rapid-exchange limit, the slow collective line has the parametric width

$$
\Gamma_{\rm narrow}\sim\frac{\operatorname{Var}(\omega)}{R}.
$$

This is motional averaging: the frequency itself changes in time. The relation is intentionally a scaling law because the source scan required visual reading and the prefactor depends on the exchange model. It is not the static spectral-gap formula proposed for glued trees.
