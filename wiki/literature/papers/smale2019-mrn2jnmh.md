## Scope and technical content

Realizes a collective Heisenberg model in mode-space using ultracold fermionic potassium. Above a critical interaction strength an interaction-induced many-body gap protects longitudinal magnetization from an inhomogeneous axial field, changing rapid dephasing into long-lived order and directly motivating the project's gap-versus-disorder diagnostic.

## Main result
