## Scope and technical content

Explores a high-cooperativity nanophotonic cavity containing a strongly driven, inhomogeneous rare-earth ensemble. Collective interference creates a narrow transparency window and pulsed excitation spans fast superradiant to slow subradiant emission, showing that drive, disorder, and cavity feedback can reorganize rather than simply erase the dark sector.
