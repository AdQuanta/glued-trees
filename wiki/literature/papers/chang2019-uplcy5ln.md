## Scope and technical content

Develops a many-body theory of optical absorption in doped two-dimensional semiconductors where an exciton is dressed by a Fermi sea. Exciton-polaron and trion-like features emerge from repeated carrier scattering, and oscillator strength and line shape evolve with density in ways not captured by an isolated three-body complex.
