## Scope and technical content

Compares two nonequilibrium criticality notions in a power-law Ising chain: a transition in the time-averaged magnetization and singularities in return probability to the ground-state manifold. The order-parameter transition occurs only for sufficiently long-range interactions, whereas return-rate singularities persist more broadly and coincide with it only in the long-range regime.
