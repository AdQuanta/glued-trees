## Scope and technical content

Gives an explicit general solution of time-dependent BCS and central-spin models using integrability and spectral-polynomial methods. Generic motion is stable and quasiperiodic with many frequencies, while special initial conditions reduce the number of frequencies and yield simpler collective oscillations; the construction links pairing and spin-bath dynamics.
