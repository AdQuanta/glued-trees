## Scope and technical content

Reviews many-body methods for coherent nonlinear spectroscopy of electronic and vibrational excitons. Cumulant expansions treat Gaussian diagonal and off-diagonal fluctuations, while nonlinear exciton equations close the response hierarchy and isolate phase-matched multidimensional signals without diagonalizing the full many-exciton Hilbert space.
