## Scope and technical content

Realizes a triangular-lattice Hubbard simulator in aligned WSe2/WS2 and probes it optically as carrier density and magnetic field vary. Half filling shows Mott insulation with antiferromagnetic Curie-Weiss behavior, while higher filling suggests a transition toward weak ferromagnetism.

## Main derivation or method

## Main result

Half filling shows Mott insulation with antiferromagnetic Curie-Weiss behavior, while higher filling suggests a transition toward weak ferromagnetism.
