## Scope and technical content

Observes cavity protection in a superconducting resonator strongly coupled to an inhomogeneously broadened NV spin ensemble. Increasing collective coupling produces long-lived oscillations and linewidth suppression consistent with spectral separation from dark spin modes, supplying an experimental bright/dark leakage analogue.
