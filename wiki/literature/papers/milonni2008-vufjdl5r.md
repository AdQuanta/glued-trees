## Scope and technical content

Compares linear polarizabilities of two- and three-level atoms and reconciles density-matrix and oscillator-style derivations. It tracks resonant, antiresonant, and damping terms explicitly, showing when a reduced few-level model reproduces the optical theorem and when omitted levels affect off-resonant response.
