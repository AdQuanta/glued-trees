## Scope and technical content

Shows that a weak quasi-local deformation of the Rydberg-blockade Hamiltonian yields nearly perfect revivals from a Néel state while the bulk spectrum remains thermal. The special subspace realizes an approximate SU(2) algebra and contains atypical scar eigenstates, illustrating coherent dynamics protected by algebraic structure rather than a global spectral gap.

## Main result

The special subspace realizes an approximate SU(2) algebra and contains atypical scar eigenstates, illustrating coherent dynamics protected by algebraic structure rather than a global spectral gap.
