## Scope and technical content

Maps quantum quenches in fully connected Bose-Hubbard, generalized Jaynes-Cummings, and Ising models to low-dimensional classical Hamiltonian motion within the permutation-symmetric sector. Dynamical transitions arise when trajectories encounter separatrices or unstable fixed points, clarifying why long-time order parameters can change nonanalytically in mean-field limits.
