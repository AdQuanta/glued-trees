## Scope and technical content

Creates electrically injected interlayer excitons in MoSe2/WSe2 double layers and observes a density threshold, charge-balance cusp, and super-Poissonian critical fluctuations in electroluminescence. The signatures persist to about 100 K and are consistent with condensation, though direct counterflow superfluidity and long-range coherence remain future tests.
