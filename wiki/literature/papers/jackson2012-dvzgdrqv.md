## Scope and technical content

Simulates onsite disorder on the full glued-tree Hilbert space rather than imposing column-correlated disorder. Weak disorder primarily leaks amplitude out of the column-symmetric channel, intermediate disorder crosses toward diffusive transport, and only strong disorder produces the Cayley-tree-like localization transition; this leakage/decay regime is the direct baseline for passive layer protection.
