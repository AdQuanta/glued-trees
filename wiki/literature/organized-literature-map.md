## Purpose

This is the canonical literature map. It is strategic rather than collection-based: each paper has exactly one primary route defined by how it can help a glued-trees paper. Detailed technical accounts, derivations or methods, results, project relevance, evidence, and cross-links live in the linked paper pages—not in this map.

A paper may be useful in several ways, but it is listed once only. Secondary relevance is carried by the cross-links inside its dedicated page and concept pages. This prevents duplicated inventories and makes each route auditable.
