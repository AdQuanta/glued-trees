## Abstract

We develop a generic method to compute the dynamics induced by quenches in completely connected quantum systems. These models are expected to provide a mean-ﬁeld description at least of the short-time dynamics of ﬁnitedimensional systems. We apply our method to the Bose–Hubbard model, to a generalized Jaynes–Cummings model, and to the Ising model in a transverse ﬁeld. We ﬁnd that the quantum evolution can be mapped onto a classical eﬀective dynamics, which involves only a few intensive observables. For some special parameters of the quench, peculiar dynamical transitions occur. They result from singularities of the classical eﬀective dynamics and are reminiscent of the transition recently found in the fermionic Hubbard model. Finally, we discuss the generality of our results and possible extensions.
