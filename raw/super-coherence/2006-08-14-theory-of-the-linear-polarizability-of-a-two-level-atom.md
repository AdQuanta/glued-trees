## Abstract

The polarizability of a two-level atom in the presence of radiative damping alone is derived by a Green function calculation. The rotating-wave approximation is not made, either in the interaction of the atom with the incident light or in its interaction with the scattered optical ﬁeld. In contrast to previous work, the derivation takes account of the modiﬁcation of the system ground state brought about by the non-rotating-wave part of the latter interaction. The ﬁnal expression satisﬁes the optical theorem in addition to the other general requirements on the polarizability.
